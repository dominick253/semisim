## Measured defect this slice exposed, not fixed here

tests/scratch/wheel_spin_probe.gd drives the harness at full throttle for 10 s
and samples every second. The combination accelerates to 6.26 m/s while
wheel_omega_rad_s stays 0.000 rad/s and the drive wheel node accumulates 0.0
degrees. The drive wheel slip ratio sits pegged at -1.0000 for the whole run,
which is the signature of a wheel that is not rolling at all:

  t=1.0 speed=0.70 omega=0.000 slip=-0.6858 node_spin=0.0 deg
  t=2.0 speed=1.38 omega=0.000 slip=-1.0000 node_spin=0.0 deg
  t=5.0 speed=3.64 omega=0.000 slip=-1.0000 node_spin=0.0 deg
  t=10.0 speed=6.26 omega=-0.000 slip=-1.0000 node_spin=0.0 deg

Cause: scripts/vehicles/combination_controller.gd line 578 integrates
wheel_omega_rad_s from net torque, and the delivered contact force
(delivered_n, line 543) equals the torque demand whenever the friction ellipse
is not saturated, so net torque is about zero every tick and the integrator
never accumulates. The visual wheels therefore never turn in normal driving and
the longitudinal slip readout is wrong for the drive axle. This predates the
visual swap (the placeholder wheels never turned either) and is tracked as the
next slice, AS4d.
