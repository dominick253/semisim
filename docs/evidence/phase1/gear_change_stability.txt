## The two defects (corrected diagnosis)

The brief's earlier "283 km/h energy injector" claim was wrong. The dead child's
own cumulative-work audit shows what actually happened. At HEAD the probe
seeds the loaded combination at 22.2222 m/s (80 km/h) in the natural high gear
and hard-downshifts to gear 1 at t=2. Two independent defects then corrupt the
run:

Defect 1 -- the test ground has no edge containment. The harness's single
ground box was 300 m on a side (150 m half-extent). The probe coasts ~22 m/s, so
it crosses z = -150 (the box edge) at t=12 s, leaves the ground, and free-falls:
the suspension cumulative work jumps to -31.37 MJ in one second and then goes
flat, and wheel_omega freezes at 10.97 rad/s with no contact. Every number after
t=12 (work, wheel rate, speed) is bogus.

Defect 2 -- a gear change at speed over-revs the rigid driveline into a permanent
engine shutdown. `engine_rpm` (combination_controller.gd, `_step`) is the max of
the vehicle-speed and wheel-rate rpm, both through the current gear ratio -- the
driveline is an always-rigid coupling (D51), so the rpm path does not respect the
clutch pedal. Selecting gear 1 (ratio 4.834) at 22.2222 m/s maps the 39.47 rad/s
drive-axle rate to ~6,742 rpm, which the driveline clamps to the pinned 1,900 rpm
shutdown (ENGINE_SHUTDOWN_RPM) on the first tick, and `engine_running` goes
false. The engine then stays dead for the rest of the run: the rpm remains
clamped at 1,900 (the wheel is still turning through the too-low ratio), which is
above the 700 rpm idle floor the D54 restart rule needs, so `engine_running`
never returns to true. The over-rev and the map-edge free-fall are the two real
defects; the "energy injector" was a misread of the post-free-fall work audit.

## The fix (no pinned constant changed)

Over-rev guard (scripts/physics/transmission.gd). Option 2 of the brief: refuse
a ratio change that would exceed redline while engaged. `select_gear` now calls
`_overrev_reason(speed_m_s, new_gear)` before committing the shift. That helper
computes the engine rpm the candidate ratio would demand through the same
`DrivelineModel` conversion as D51 (GEAR_RATIOS[candidate] * FINAL_DRIVE_RATIO
* RPM_PER_DRIVE_AXLE_RAD_S, using the pinned TIRE_DRIVE for the wheel rate), and
returns a non-empty reason if it would exceed ENGINE_REDLINE_RPM (1800). If
refused, the gear does not change and `last_reject_reason` is set to "SHIFT
WOULD EXCEED REDLINE" (the reason string is a local const, REASON_OVERREV, in
transmission.gd -- scripts/input/noop_rules.gd is not in the brief's allowed
files, so it is not touched).

Why not an rpm slew limit or reflected-inertia option: with the D51 coupling
always rigid, a ratio change is instantaneous and the rpm would still snap past
redline on the first tick (the slew limit would clamp the rpm but the shift would
still be committed, and the D54 restart would still be blocked while the rpm sat
clamped at 1,900). Refusing the over-rev ratio change is the smallest honest rule
that keeps the engine alive, and it matches what a real transmission/driver does
for a shift that would instantly past-redline the engine. The guard is
deliberately not gated on the clutch pedal because the D51 coupling is always
rigid -- the clutch does not decouple the rpm path. A downshift that stays inside
the rev range is unaffected (e.g. gear 4 at 19 m/s maps to ~1,538 rpm and is
accepted).

Test ground (tests/sim/combination_rest_harness.gd). The ground half-extent is
raised 150 -> 800 m (the box is 1600 m on a side). Sizing: the worst-case probe
distance is v_max x t = 22.2222 m/s x 30 s = 666.67 m, plus the 6.5 m
steer-axle overhang ahead of the drive-axle origin = 673.17 m; 800 m keeps every
planned run at least ~127 m from the edge. (The 120-tick city golden run, 10 s,
covers only 222.2 m.) A new `out_of_bounds()` reports true when either the
tractor's or the trailer's center has left the ground box -- a report, not a
containment, so a silent departure becomes a visible test failure.

## The unit test (tests/unit/test_gear_change_stability.gd, RED first)

Two tests, both harness-based (the same harness, cargo, and 120 Hz step as the
probe):

  1. test_hard_downshift_at_speed_keeps_engine_alive_and_on_ground: seed at
     22.2222 m/s (80 km/h) in the natural high gear (gear 5), settle to the idle
     floor, close throttle, and `select_gear(1, ...)`. Assert the shift is
     refused (gear stays 5), the engine is alive with rpm < 1,900, then integrate
     for up to 10 s (120 ticks/s) asserting `out_of_bounds()` is false on every
     tick and the engine is alive at the end.
  2. test_legitimate_downshift_inside_rev_range_still_works: seed at 19 m/s in
     gear 5 and downshift to gear 4. The candidate rpm (~1,538) is inside the
     rev range, so the shift is accepted (assert the returned bool is true and
     gear == 4), and `out_of_bounds()` stays false over 3.3 s.

RED first (before the guard): test 1 failed 4 asserts (gear landed on 1, not 5;
rpm read 1,900, not < 1,900; the engine was dead 10 s after the downshift) and
test 2 failed (the gear-4 shift was not accepted as the engine had already been
killed by the over-rev). All 1,200 `out_of_bounds()` checks passed even in the
RED state, because the ground-extent fix and the over-rev guard are independent.
GREEN (after the guard): 2/2 passing, 1,208 asserts.

## The measurements (tests/scratch/downshift_probe.gd, 30 s, 120 Hz, loaded)

The probe was rewritten to establish the pre-shift state in gear 5 (manual) at
80 km/h, so the guarded downshift is the only over-rev event (not the seed), and
to route the downshift through the real `select_gear` path (it previously
assigned `transmission.gear = 1` directly). It now prints a `SELECT` line
(accepted/rejected + reason) and an `oob=` field on every sampled tick.

  PRE    gear=5 range=0 rpm=1390 omega=39.29 speed=22.12   (engine alive)
  SELECT gear=1 accepted=false reason=SHIFT WOULD EXCEED REDLINE (gear=5)

Sampled every second (gear, run, rpm, speed, oob):

  t=0.0  gear=5 run=1 rpm=1390 speed=22.12 oob=0
  t=2.0  gear=5 run=1 rpm=1335 speed=21.25 oob=0     <- the refused downshift here
  t=5.0  gear=5 run=1 rpm= 992 speed=19.88 oob=0
  t=10.0 gear=5 run=1 rpm= 700 speed=17.41 oob=0
  t=20.0 gear=5 run=1 rpm= 700 speed=12.21 oob=0
  t=30.0 gear=5 run=1 rpm= 700 speed= 6.58 oob=0     (coasting to a stop)

  run=1 (engine alive) at every sampled tick over the full 30 s; oob=0 at every
  sampled tick; the engine_rpm peaks at 1,390 (PRE) and decays to the 700 idle
  floor as the rig coasts -- it never touches the 1,800 redline or the 1,900

The work-audit window (t=10.5-13.5, where the rig crosses the OLD 150 m edge at
