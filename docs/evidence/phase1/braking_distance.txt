CITYHAUL AS4k: the 80 km/h full-brake-plus-retarder stop lands in the spec window
=================================================================================

Repo state: on main. Touched files: tests/unit/test_braking_distance.gd (new,
2 tests), docs/evidence/phase1/braking_distance.txt (this file), and one new row
(D56) in docs/DEVIATIONS.md. The probe tests/scratch/stop_distance_probe.gd was

## The target (physics-spec section 5)

"Full service brake plus retarder from 80 km/h on dry stops in 24 to 34 m; a
braked rig at rest stays at rest (no creep, no reversal)."

The target did not exist as a test and did not hold as written: the parent's probe
measured 40.44 m in 7.45 s (average deceleration 6.10 m/s^2 = 0.62 g). This file
shows what 40.44 m actually measures and where the in-window number comes from.

## Method (tests/scratch/stop_distance_probe.gd)

The probe loads tests/sim/combination_rest_harness.tscn, settles 240 physics
frames (2 s at the 120 Hz fixed step, confirmed in project.godot), seeds the
combination at 22.2222 m/s (80 km/h) along the tractor heading on both bodies
(the harness leaves it at rest), applies the chosen controls, then integrates
until speed < 0.05 m/s and accumulates distance from
`tractor.global_position.distance_to(start)` per tick. Every variant prints a
per-second force row: gear, engine/retarder on, retarder N, per-axle delivered
brake N (front/drive/back), total longitudinal N, drive N, slip, drive-axle
adhesion N, and a final STOP row. The same harness, cargo and 120 Hz step are used
by the pinned unit test, so probe and test measure the same thing.

Set-velocity (not driven-up) is the seeding method, and the reason is measured,
not assumed. The brief's Step 2 wanted a driven-up variant (accelerate under
throttle 1.0 in the highest reasonable gear to 80 km/h, then brake). Three ways
to reach "80 km/h with the retarder live" were measured and all fail for physical
reasons:

  1. Drive up in a retarder gear from rest: in gear 4 the loaded rig cannot
     launch from idle. throttle 1.0, clutch engaged, the drive force asymptotes
     at 9,563 N and the rig creeps to 1.91 m/s (6.9 km/h) in 60 s, never reaching
     80 km/h. A truck cannot launch from rest in a high gear at idle.
  2. Drive up in auto: the gearbox climbs 1 -> 5 and reaches 80 km/h in gear 5
     (the top of the high range, engine at ~1,385 rpm, below the 1,550 upshift
     threshold). In gear 5 the spec's own "gears 1-4" retarder rule keeps the
     retarder off, so the auto-driven stop is service-brake-only (40.44 m at
     default cargo / 51.01 m at full load, below).
  3. Drive up in auto, then hard-downshift into gear 4 at 80 km/h: the rigid
     driveline has no rev-matcher, so the downshift (engine 1,385 -> ~1,800 rpm)
     destabilizes it and the combination RUNS AWAY, 22.25 -> 90.79 m/s in 30 s
     (2,180 m), never braking.

So "80 km/h + full service brake + full retarder" -- the exact scenario the spec
target names -- is only reproducible by seeding the velocity in a retarder-allowed
gear. The set-velocity number is the measured one; the auto number is the
cross-check.

kg). Four same-process repeats (probe) plus the GUT process:

  probe r1   28.69 m   387 ticks   3.23 s

  => 28.53-28.69 m, mean 28.61 m, spread 0.16 m. IN the 24-34 m window with 4.5 m
     of floor margin and 5.4 m of ceiling margin.

## The diagnosis (with numbers, no constants touched)

The retarder is applied in CombinationController._step only when
`retarder_allowed = engine_running and gear > 0 and gear <= RETARDER_MAX_GEAR
and speed > RETARDER_MIN_SPEED`, and RETARDER_MAX_GEAR = 4 (PHYSICS_CONSTANTS,
pinned and mirrored to physics-spec line 94: "active only in gears 1-4"). So the
retarder is live only in gears 1-4.

The auto-transmission at 80 km/h (22.22 m/s) sits in gear 5: the engine rpm in
gear 5 is 63.18 * 22.22 = 1,404 rpm, above the 1,200 downshift floor and below
the 1,550 upshift ceiling, so auto holds gear 5. Gear 5 is one above
RETARDER_MAX_GEAR, so `retarder_allowed` is false and the "plus retarder" stop is
never actually applied. The per-second rows confirm it:

  auto (parent)  t=0.5  gear=5  retN=0        Ftot=-146,168 N  (brake only)
  auto (parent)  t=1.0  gear=5  retN=0        Ftot=-146,168 N
  auto (parent)  t=1.5  gear=5  retN=0        Ftot=-146,168 N
  => the whole high-speed stop is a ~146 kN service-brake stop; the retarder's
     250 kN is never on the ground. 146 kN on the 20,000 kg default cargo gives
     40.44 m, matching the parent byte for byte.

The in-window number needs the two things the target implies but the parent's
probe did not set:

  (a) The retarder actually applied. That requires a retarder-allowed gear (1-4).
      Holding gear 4 turns retN from 0 to 250,000 N on the first tick.
  (b) The loaded combination. The static axle shares carry the cargo mass:
      20,000 kg -> 67.9 / 60.2 / 107.3 kN (steer/drive/trailer adhesion);
      29,000 kg -> 82.7 / 108.1 / 150.6 kN. More load, more adhesion, more
      torque-limited brake force per axle, a shorter stop. The D20 design claim
      names "the loaded combination" explicitly, and T14 in
      test_physics_rules.gd already asserts the 29,000 kg shares (7,031 / 9,182 /
      12,787 kg).

With both set (gear 4, 29,000 kg), the per-second force table is:

  t=0.5  gear=4  retN=250,000  brk[f 9,000 / d 12,000 / b 10,000]  Ftot=-332,594 N
  t=1.0  gear=4  retN=250,000  brk[f 9,000 / d 12,000 / b 10,000]  Ftot=-332,811 N
  t=1.5  gear=4  retN=250,000  brk[f 9,000 / d 12,000 / b 10,000]  Ftot=-397,137 N

  => ~332-397 kN total (250 kN retarder + torque-limited brake + ~61 kN engine
     braking) on the loaded combination: 28.6 m. The D13/D20 design intent
     ("stops in 27..28 m from 80 km/h at stage 5 plus service brake") is
     reproduced: 28.6 m is within their 27-28 m statement to < 1 m.

So the physics reaches the window in the scenario the target describes. The 40.44
m was not a physics defect; it was a measurement of a different scenario (auto /
gear 5 / default cargo) where the spec's own retarder gate keeps the "plus
retarder" term off. Nothing in the brake chain is wrong: the retarder force
reaches both bodies (D20's 8-tire distribution), the brake torque reaches the
axle (demand = torque / radius, per-wheel, torque-limited then adhesion-capped),
the units are N and N m throughout, and the per-axle force scales with the
correct static load.

## What the unit test pins (tests/unit/test_braking_distance.gd)

Two tests, matching the two spec sentences:

  1. test_full_brake_plus_retarder_stops_in_spec_window: full load
     (CARGO_MASS_MAX_KG), transmission held in gear 4 (the top of the "gears 1-4"
     window, so the retarder is live), seeded at 22.2222 m/s, throttle 0 /
     brake 1 / retarder stage 5, integrate to < 0.05 m/s and assert 24.0 <=
     distance <= 34.0. Measured 28.569 m in the GUT process (PASS). The window
     assert is used rather than an equality so the test pins the target's bound,
     with the margin documented above, not a brittle point value.
  2. test_braked_rig_at_rest_stays_at_rest: full brake at rest, 600 ticks, assert
     max speed < 0.1 m/s and max displacement < 0.05 m (no creep, no reversal).
     This pins the D37 brake-hold rule (a braked rig below 0.05 m/s is held at
     rest); the retarder is gated off below RETARDER_MIN_SPEED so the brake is the
     load here. (PASS)

## Follow-ups recorded per the brief (NOT fixed in this slice)

  1. AirSystem.consume_psi() has no production caller, so air pressure stays at
     100 psi in play and the 60/40 psi spring-brake ladder is decorative. Wiring
     the consumption into _step (throttle/compressor load) is the next slice
     (AS4l). This slice only asserts the at-rest HOLD (D37), which does not depend
     on the air model.
  2. The coupler has no RELEASE input: it is built locked (locked = true) and the
     trailer's linear limits (D36) keep it coupled, so "detach the trailer" is
     unreachable. A release path (a coupler_unlock action driving the joint's
     linear limits / lock state) is a follow-up, not part of this slice.

## What this slice does and does not fix

It pins the spec section 5 stopping targets as unit tests and documents why the
naive reading (auto mode, default cargo) misses them. It does NOT change any
production physics: the in-window stop already exists in the loaded combination
in a retarder-allowed gear, so there is no code to fix -- the parent's 40.44 m was
a scenario artifact (gear 5 gates the retarder off; default cargo carries less
adhesion). The residual, stated plainly: a driver who brakes at 80 km/h in the
cruising gear (5) in auto, with the default load, will still stop in ~40 m, not
24-34, because that is the service-brake-only stop the spec's own "gears 1-4"
retarder rule produces. The 24-34 m window is the loaded-combination, retarder-on
number, which is what D13/D20 always claimed.
