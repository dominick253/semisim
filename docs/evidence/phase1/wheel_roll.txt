## The defect and the fix

`wheel_omega_rad_s` was integrated from the contact torque imbalance. The
torque-demand tire model delivers exactly the demand while the tyre grips, so
the imbalance is about zero every tick and the integrator never accumulated.
The fix sets the rate from the no-slip relation `v_forward_wheel / radius`
while `absf(demand_n) <= adhesion`, and keeps the original integrator only when
the friction ellipse saturates. The tire model, `delivered_n`, the ABS branch
and every PHYSICS_CONSTANTS value are untouched. Visual spin is now scaled per
axle by `drive_radius / wheel_radius`, so the 0.554 m steer tyres no longer
under-spin by 1.6 percent against the 0.563 m drive and bogie tyres.

Reading: at 6.26 m/s the no-slip rate is 6.26 / 0.563 = 11.12 rad/s and the
controller reports 11.120. Longitudinal slip moved from the locked-wheel -1.0
to about -0.0005. The node_spin column is a single angle_to read from the start
pose, so it wraps at 180 degrees; it is a non-zero proof of rotation, not a
cumulative angle. The cumulative claim is carried by
tests/unit/test_wheel_rolling.gd, which sums per-tick rotation and requires
spin times radius to equal travelled distance within 2 percent.

## Saturation reachability, measured, and the gap it leaves

tests/scratch/wheel_saturation_probe.gd drives to 2.65 m/s and pins the brake
pedal at 1.0 on the dry yard surface:

  t=0.0 speed=2.65 omega=4.711 v_over_r=4.709 slip=-0.0029 abs=false
        normal_n=57090 adhesion_n=65654 brake_nm=12000 demand_n=21314

The pinned drive brake torque (BRAKE_TORQUE_DRIVE_NM 12000) produces a 21,314 N
demand against a 65,654 N adhesion limit, so the friction ellipse does not
saturate on dry and the integrator branch never runs. `WheelRig.mu()` returns
`tire["mu_dry"]` unconditionally, so the mu_wet 0.70 and mu_ice 0.25 columns in
PHYSICS_CONSTANTS are dead data and no in-game situation currently reaches
saturation, ABS engagement or the spin-up and lock transient. Wiring surface
dependent mu is the next slice, AS4e.
