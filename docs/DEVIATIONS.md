# CITYHAUL Deviations Log

Every spec item that is technically impossible on this engine or host, with
field, spec value, final value, and reason. Never silently substitute.

Static-load closure check: with D1, D2, D3, D4, D5 applied, the corrected model
reproduces the spec's own T14 steer axle targets: empty 5,859 kg (spec 5,886,
0.5 percent off) and full 7,416 kg (spec 7,417, 0.02 percent off). The spec's
original numbers cannot be produced by any physically valid geometry.

| D9 | Trailer empty box inertia | Ix 415,000 / Iy 331,000 / Iz 113,600 | Ix 161,809 / Iy 156,060 / Iz 13,576 | Same as D8 for 7,000 kg over 2.59 x 4.07 x 16.15 m. |
| D10 | T6 expected loaded CoM | (0, 1.794, -6.58) | (0, 1.671, +6.41) | The spec value is not the mass-weighted average of the spec's own empty and cargo CoM values (y: 7,000*1.55 + 29,000*1.70 over 36,000 = 1.671). z follows D4/D5. |
| D11 | T7 expected loaded trailer Iy | 486,000 kg m^2 | 802,592 kg m^2 (asserted with the spec's +/- 10 percent band) | The spec says inertia is recomputed "as a uniform box of the combined mass". | D12 | Suspension spring rates | steer 55,000 N/m, drive/trailer 65,000 N/m, ARB 30/45/35 kN/m | steer 1,100,000 N/m, drive/trailer 1,300,000 N/m, ARB 600/900/700 kN/m | The spec rates sag 0.5 to 1.7 m under the spec masses, far beyond the pinned +/- 0.12 m travel; the suspension would bottom out at rest. | D13 | Retarder units | "50, 100, 150, 200, 250 kN" | 50,000 to 250,000 N | The spec header says force in kN but a 250 kN retarder alone would decelerate the combination at 5.6 m/s^2, more than the entire brake system. | D14 | Steer axle legal cap | 7,711 kg applied to the steer axle | 7,711 kg kept as pinned, applied to the steer axle | 7,711 kg (17,000 lb) is the US limit for a single axle group; the US steer axle limit is 5,443 kg (12,000 lb). The pinned cap is retained as written; the note is recorded so Phase 2 legal-load checks use the right numbers. |
| D15 | Steering max angle vs turning radius target | 35 deg full lock AND curb-to-curb radius 7.6 m | 35 deg kept | A 6.5 m wheelbase at 35 deg gives a 9.3 m turning radius. 7.6 m would need 40.5 deg. The pinned 35 deg is kept; the radius target is recorded as unachievable with the pinned wheelbase and will be re-checked in Phase 2 tuning. |
| D16 | Air drag and rolling resistance | not specified | CdA 3.0 m^2, Crr 0.008 | The spec has no coast-down mechanism at all, so a throttled combination would accelerate without bound below the governor. Standard truck drag and rolling resistance added as the only way to make the top-speed governor and idle behavior meaningful. Logged here and in DRIVING-FEEL.md. |
| D17 | Kingpin joint class | Generic6DOFSpringConstraint3D | Generic6DOFJoint3D | Generic6DOFSpringConstraint3D is a Godot 3.x class and does not exist in Godot 4.7. Generic6DOFJoint3D is the 4.x equivalent with per-axis limits, motors and springs, and it is the class that the Jolt backend supports. |
| D18 | Automatic shift rules | upshift 1550 rpm, downshift 1150 rpm only | same thresholds, plus no-lug and no-low-range-above-15-km/h guards | With the pinned ratios, an unguarded auto downshift lugs the engine below idle (rpm clamps at idle and the shift oscillates), and an auto upshift into low range at speed drops rpm under idle the same way. Guards added; thresholds unchanged. |
| D19 | Strike impulse threshold | 15 kN contact impulse | contact event with speed above 1 m/s (proxy) | Jolt in Godot 4.7 does not expose per-contact impulse magnitudes to GDScript; RigidBody3D.body_entered carries no force. The 15 kN rule is applied through a speed proxy on wall/building contacts. |
| D20 | Retarder as a tire force | not specified | driveline drag distributed over all 8 tires in proportion to static load share | The spec calls the retarder a driveline device but gives no path for it to reach the ground. Applying it only to the drive axle would lock 2 tires and skid them; spreading it by load share reproduces the S3 stop window. |
| D21 | Body collider height | spec boxes span ground to roof | collider bottoms raised above the wheel contact plane | With colliders touching the ground, the body boxes outrun the raycast suspension and the wheels lose contact at rest. Colliders are raised so the suspension is the sole support; body collisions still apply for yard strikes. |
| D22 | Kingpin joint torque ceiling | not specified (Godot-Jolt default force_limit 0.0) | 2.0e7 N m on all angular limits | Godot-Jolt angular limits default to force_limit 0.0, and a zero ceiling makes the angular constraint apply no torque at all: the trailer free-falls on a pendulum about the kingpin (tests/probe_angular.gd). The ceiling must exceed the fifth wheel moment (about 1.7e6 N m fully loaded). |
| D27 | Off-tracking target | loaded combination under 1.2 m on a 15 m radius circle at 12 km/h | 3.82 m geometric shortfall on the 15 m circle | With the pinned 6.5 m tractor wheelbase and 10.0 m kingpin-to-bogie distance the bicycle model puts the bogie at sqrt(15^2 - 10^2) = 11.18 m while the kingpin follows 15.00 m, a 3.82 m shortfall (5.14 m measured against the 16.32 m steer axle path). 1.2 m off-tracking would need a kingpin-to-bogie distance under 5.9 m on that circle, which contradicts the pinned 53 ft trailer geometry. The target is achievable only on a wider circle: the shortfall approaches 100/(2R), so under 1.2 m requires a radius above about 42 m. The geometric value is pinned as OFF_TRACKING_GEOMETRIC_M_15M and asserted instead. |
| D29 | HUD flash expiry | (unspecified duration) | 3.0 s of simulated time, advanced by `Hud.refresh(dt)` rather than a Godot Timer | A wall-clock Timer does not tick deterministically in headless runs, so the reason flash could expire at different points between runs and break the acceptance evidence. The flash countdown rides the same fixed tick as the simulation. |

When the GLB lands, the rig reads the real empties and these constants become the fallback. |
| D32 | Free camera orbit input | "right stick or mouse drag" (section 10), no rate given | `FREE_ORBIT_RATE_RAD_S` 1.4 rad/s with the pinned gamepad deadzone | The spec pins the 20 m radius but not the orbit speed, and an unbounded rate makes the camera uncontrollable. 1.4 rad/s is one full revolution in about 4.5 s. |
