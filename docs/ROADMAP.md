# CITYHAUL Roadmap

## Phase 1: driving core (IN PROGRESS)

Scope: test yard, hybrid physics (tractor + trailer + kingpin constraint),
Pacejka tires, engine/transmission/retarder, air brakes, coupling and landing
gear, 3 challenges with scoring, 4 cameras, HUD, procedural audio, GUT unit
tests, sim scenarios, golden determinism, CI.

Exit gate: 20 acceptance IDs (A1..A20) with evidence in `docs/evidence/phase1/`,
`docs/phase1-end-state.md` written, and `docs/phase2-prompt.md` written and
committed.

## Phase 2: world and cargo loop (NOT STARTED)

Scope: the single big city (block grid, tight streets, alleys, the yards),
3 small towns with farms, cargo and delivery game loop, AI traffic, save and
settings expansion, full audio, Steam packaging prep (export presets,
storefront assets).

Exit gate: `docs/phase2-end-state.md` and `docs/phase3-prompt.md`.

## Phase 3: polish and Steam packaging (NOT STARTED)

Scope: component damage, separate trailer brake valve, polish, Steam
submission-ready build.

Exit gate: shippable build.
